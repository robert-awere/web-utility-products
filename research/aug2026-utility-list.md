# 41 Cloneable Single-Purpose Web Utilities for AdSense — Build List & Scoring

## TL;DR
- The best build-first bets pair high US search intent with high AdSense RPM: **auto loan calculator, take-home paycheck calculator, 401(k)/retirement calculator, calorie/TDEE calculator, and BMI calculator** top the list. Finance/insurance calculators out-earn pure-curiosity tools by roughly 10–30x on CPM — Serpzilla reports finance/insurance/investment sites "typically enjoy CPM rates ranging from $15 to $50 or higher," while "general news or entertainment websites typically receive CPM rates between $0.50 to $3."
- The dataset below covers 41 concepts split into thirds (14 Productivity, 13 Tech, 14 Science/Education), each with reference leaders, keywords, two verified domain candidates, and 1–5 scores for build ease, traffic, and monetization plus a weighted composite (composite = 0.25×ease + 0.35×traffic + 0.40×monetization).
- Domain candidates were checked for live sites; only names that resolved to real sites were removed. Registrar availability (parked/dormant) cannot be fully confirmed via search — **run a WHOIS/DNS check before buying any domain.**

## Key Findings
- **Monetization is dominated by advertiser lifetime value.** Legal, finance, insurance, and health advertisers bid highest because a converted lead is worth thousands. Adstimate's 2026 niche data puts **Legal at the highest CPC in its entire dataset — $6.70 — with RPM of $35–65**, Software/SaaS at $30–65 RPM, and Insurance at a $3.80 CPC and 2.3% CTR. This is why a technically trivial auto-loan calculator beats a harder-to-build regex tester for AdSense.
- **The category leader is calculator.net** and it is enormous: Semrush recorded **61.07M visits in July 2026** (avg. session 05:09), and Ahrefs lists its **Domain Rating at 84** with **21.6K referring websites** as of June 2026. It **ranks #2 for the head term "calculator" in the US, pulling 831.6K visits from that single keyword.** Ahrefs' own case study notes calculator.net runs "216 pages in their /calculator/ parent subfolder, which has total traffic of 8.6m" across "77.2k calculator-themed keywords." It is beatable only on individual long-tail sub-tools with better UX, speed, and depth — not head-on.
- **Highest-traffic terms** in this set are internet speed test (~1M–5M+/mo US, est.), BMI calculator, unit converter, GPA calculator, and paycheck calculator (all very high volume). **Highest-CPC terms** are auto loan calculator, invoice generator, alimony calculator, and 401(k) calculator. Newor Media pegs finance/insurance CPCs at "$15-45 (U.S.); $5-20 (global)" and recommends "comparison guides, calculators, and how-to articles" as the format.
- **The sweet spot for AdSense is finance/health calculators that are still simple client-side builds** — large volume, strong RPM, low engineering cost. Legal calculators (alimony, child support) carry the highest RPM but are harder to build (per-state formulas) and are YMYL-sensitive.
- **Client-side-only tools win on cost and privacy.** Nearly every leader in this list runs entirely in the browser (no server, no file upload), which minimizes hosting cost and eases AdSense approval.

## Details

### Scoring method
- **Ease of build (1–5):** 5 = trivial static/JS, single formula; 1 = complex logic, per-jurisdiction data, or server infrastructure.
- **Traffic (1–5):** search-intent volume, English/global/US-weighted.
- **Monetization (1–5):** expected AdSense RPM, weighted toward finance/insurance/legal/health advertiser demand.
- **Composite = 0.25×Ease + 0.35×Traffic + 0.40×Monetization** (monetization weighted highest per the brief).
- **Volume figures are estimates** (order-of-magnitude) triangulated from SEO sources and the category leaders' keyword profiles; validate in Google Keyword Planner/Ahrefs/Semrush before committing budget.

### Top 5 build-first candidates (by composite, monetization-weighted)

1. **Auto loan / car payment calculator (P2 — 4.7).** Trivial single-formula build, huge intent (~100K–300K/mo US est.), and top-tier CPC from lenders — live competitors bidding on this space include calculator.net, bankrate.com and nerdwallet.com, and ClickPatrol notes mortgage/loan keywords "often exceed $50–$100 per click." Differentiate with trade-in, tax-by-state, and amortization-schedule depth that calculator.net under-serves on mobile.
2. **Take-home / paycheck calculator (P1 — 4.4).** Very high volume with recurring seasonal spikes (tax season, new-job). RPM lifted by payroll/fintech advertisers. Win with 2026 brackets, per-state accuracy, and offer-comparison mode.
3. **Calorie / TDEE calculator (S14 — 4.4).** Massive health-search volume and strong RPM from weight-loss, supplement, and telehealth advertisers. Simple Mifflin-St Jeor math; win with macro splits and goal-date projections.
4. **401(k) / retirement calculator (P3 — 4.4).** High-value finance advertisers (brokerages, annuities). Straightforward compounding math; win with employer-match modeling and SECURE 2.0 catch-up rules.
5. **BMI calculator (S13 — 4.2).** Among the highest-volume terms anywhere and the simplest possible build. Lower CPC than finance but volume compensates; win with body-fat/ideal-weight companion outputs and clean mobile UX.

**Honorable mentions:** Invoice generator (4.1 — high SaaS CPC; Adstimate puts Software/SaaS RPM at $30–65), QR code generator (4.0), image compressor (4.0). Alimony/child support (3.6) carry the single highest RPM in the dataset — Adstimate calls Legal "the highest CPC in the entire dataset at $6.70... a RPM of $35–$65 is among the strongest available to any publisher" — but are gated by per-state formula complexity and YMYL scrutiny. Good phase-2 plays once you have domain authority.

## Recommendations
- **Phase 1 (build now):** Ship P2 (auto loan), P1 (paycheck), and S14 (calorie/TDEE). These are 1–3 day builds, all client-side, and hit the finance/health RPM sweet spot. Register the .io candidates after a WHOIS check; keep the second candidate as backup.
- **Phase 2 (scale once indexed):** Add P3 (401k), S13 (BMI), P4 (invoice), P12 (QR), T3 (image compressor). Cross-link them into a small tool "hub" to build topical authority and internal-link equity — mirroring how calculator.net stacks 216 interlinked calculator pages to compound traffic.
- **Phase 3 (high-RPM, high-effort):** Tackle P5/P6 (alimony/child support) with genuine per-state formulas and attorney-reviewed content — highest CPC in the set ($6.70 legal CPC per Adstimate) but requires E-E-A-T investment.
- **Build principles:** Keep everything client-side (no uploads) for cost, speed, and AdSense approval; target long-tail sub-pages (e.g., "auto loan calculator with trade-in and tax") where head terms are locked up by calculator.net/Bankrate; add a short explainer + FAQ under each tool for ad inventory and E-E-A-T.
- **Thresholds that change the plan:** If Keyword Planner shows a target head term under ~5K US/mo or CPC under ~$0.50, demote it. If a domain's WHOIS shows active registration with content, switch to the backup candidate or a new TLD. If AdSense RPM on a live tool underperforms ~$3, pivot ad placements or add an affiliate offer (lender/insurer/telehealth) alongside AdSense.
- **Avoid:** the user's already-claimed subscription cost/creep calculator concept (excluded here). Also avoid head-on cloning calculator.net's generic "calculator" — compete only on specific sub-tools.

## Caveats
- **Search volumes are estimates.** Precise Ahrefs/Semrush/Keyword Planner numbers were paywalled; figures here are order-of-magnitude, triangulated from SEO sources and leaders' keyword profiles. Verify before spending.
- **Domain availability is not guaranteed.** Search-based checks confirmed that two originally proposed names (paychecktakehome.com and formatmyjson.com) are live and were removed/replaced, and fastinvoicemaker.com appears taken (used as an app's contact domain, so P4's candidates were changed to quickinvoicemaker.io / freeinvoicenow.io). "Appears available" names may still be registered/parked/dormant — confirm via WHOIS/DNS and keep the backup ready. Several niches have near-identical live competitors on other TLDs (e.g., pdfwordcounter.io, pomofocus.io, invoicequick.com, unitconverterpro.com), raising brand-collision and SEO-competition risk; the P9 candidates were changed away from pomodorofocus.io/focustimer.app for that reason. Note one flagged adjacency: mynetspeed.co (a different TLD than the T5 candidate) has been reported as a browser-hijacker domain — prefer checkmyspeed.app for T5.
- **YMYL risk.** Finance, legal, and health tools fall under Google's "Your Money or Your Life" scrutiny; thin or inaccurate calculators can be suppressed. Add disclaimers, cite formulas/sources, and (for legal/medical) professional review.
- **AdSense policy.** Tool-only pages with little text can face approval/monetization limits; pair each tool with genuine explanatory content. RPM/CPM figures cited (Serpzilla: finance/insurance $15–50+ CPM vs entertainment $0.50–3; Adstimate: legal $35–65 RPM at $6.70 CPC, SaaS $30–65, insurance $3.80 CPC; Newor Media: finance CPC $15–45 US) are from publisher blogs, are Tier-1/US-weighted, and vary with seasonality and layout.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Short version: SpeedFlex does not have accounts, does not set cookies and does not run analytics. Your results stay in your browser unless you choose to share them.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts or sign-ups.
  • No cookies.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or third-party trackers.
  • SpeedFlex has no server of its own that receives your test results.

The speed test and Cloudflare

To measure your connection, your browser sends and receives test data directly to and from Cloudflare's speed-test endpoints at speed.cloudflare.com. As with any web request, Cloudflare can see your IP address and basic request details such as your browser's user agent. The test also asks Cloudflare for connection information (such as your network provider, approximate city and the data centre you reached) so it can be shown to you. Cloudflare's handling of that traffic is covered by Cloudflare's own privacy policy.

The pages themselves are delivered by our static hosting provider, which, like any web host, processes standard request data such as IP addresses in server logs to deliver and protect the site.

Recent results in your browser

SpeedFlex can keep a short list of your recent tests in your browser's localStorage so you can compare them later. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us, and can be removed at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. The speed test works normally if storage is blocked or unavailable.

Challenge links

When you create a challenge link, your results (download, upload, ping, a timestamp and an optional first name) are encoded in the URL hash, the part after the # sign. Browsers do not send the hash to the website's server, so this information is not sent to us. Anyone you share the link with can read it, and it may be visible to the app or service you use to share it.

Share images

Share cards are drawn in your browser. If you share or download one, it goes only where you send it.

Advertising

The site contains placeholder spaces for future advertising. They are currently inactive: no ad code is loaded and no ad network receives any data. If that changes, this policy will be updated before ads go live.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

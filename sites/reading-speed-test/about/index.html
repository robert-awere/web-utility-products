About this reading speed test

Reading Speed Test is a free, one-page tool that tells you how many words per minute you read and whether you understood what you read. There is nothing to install, no account to create and no cookies. It is built to be fast, honest about its limits, and easy to share with a friend you want to beat.

How the test works

You press Start reading, read a short non-fiction passage of roughly 230 to 290 words at your normal pace, and press I'm done reading when you finish. Five multiple-choice questions then check what you took in. Your words per minute comes from the passage length divided by your time, and your comprehension score comes from the questions, so the result card shows both together along with an effective wpm that combines them.

Where the benchmark comes from

The average adult figure shown on the result card, 238 words per minute, comes from Brysbaert, M. (2019), How many words do we read per minute? A review and meta-analysis of reading rate, published in the Journal of Memory and Language. That review combined 190 studies and found adults read non-fiction silently at about 238 wpm, fiction at about 260 wpm, and read aloud at about 183 wpm.

Read the paper on ScienceDirect

The passages are original pieces written for this site. They are plain non-fiction on everyday topics so that readers anywhere in the world have a fair shot at them.

What it is not

This is a snapshot of how you read one short passage on a screen today. It is not a reading assessment, it cannot diagnose a reading difficulty, and it is not a substitute for a teacher or specialist. If you want a steadier number, take the test on a few different passages and look at the typical result.

Contact and feedback

Spotted a mistake in a passage, a question that could be read two ways, or a bug? Please open an issue on the project's GitHub repository: github.com/robert-awere/web-utility-products/issues. Fixes to the passages or questions land there first.

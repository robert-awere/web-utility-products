Privacy policy

Effective 21 August 2026

The short version

The calculator runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter — prices, trade-ins, loan balances — never leave your device. We don't ask for accounts, names or email addresses, and we don't use our own cookies to track you.

What we don't collect

  • No calculator inputs are transmitted or stored — all math happens locally in your browser.
  • No accounts, sign-ups, or personal information.
  • No first-party tracking cookies.

Hosting logs

The site is served by Vercel, our hosting provider. Like nearly all web hosts, Vercel processes standard technical request data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver pages and protect against abuse. See the Vercel privacy policy.

Advertising

This site is supported by advertising. When ads are served by Google AdSense, Google and its partners may use cookies or device identifiers to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites, and may collect data as described in how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or via aboutads.info. Visitors in the EEA/UK will be shown a consent message before any advertising cookies are set.

Pages may also contain clearly marked affiliate links to loan-comparison services; clicking one takes you to that third party's site under its own privacy policy. We receive no personal data back from those clicks beyond aggregate referral counts.

Analytics

If we enable analytics, it will be a single privacy-respecting, cookie-free page-view counter (such as Plausible) or an equivalent disclosed here. We do not sell or share personal information.

Children

The site is a general-audience financial tool and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with an updated effective date. Questions? Contact us.

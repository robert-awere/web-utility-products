Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

The short version: your subscription list never leaves your browser. We do not ask for, collect or store the names or prices you type. Like most websites, our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, and the advertising that keeps the site free may use cookies.

Calculations happen in your browser

Every calculation on this site runs locally in your web browser using JavaScript. The subscription names, prices and billing cycles you enter are not transmitted to our servers, not stored in a database and not shared with anyone. When you close or reload the page, the list is gone.

Information we do receive

When you visit any website, the hosting infrastructure automatically records standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time of the request. These server logs are used for security and to keep the site running, and are not used to identify individual visitors.

Cookies and advertising

This site is supported by advertising served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), a consent message will be shown before advertising cookies are set.

Analytics

This site does not currently run a separate analytics product. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

Children's privacy

This site is a general-audience financial planning tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.
